## Follow-up on resume: measurement and Bridge probes

The installed Blender 5.2.0 LTS API was inspected in an isolated factory-startup process. `ClothSettings.rest_shape_key` is a KeyBlock pointer defining rest spring lengths; `use_dynamic_mesh` defaults false. A private 60-frame probe used the same 2,422-triangle panel placement, 50,312 body triangles without collider decimation, a flat-rest shape key, sewing springs, fixed pattern pins and 3 mm collision/self distances. No Solidify or export reindexing preceded measurement. It took 4.20 seconds and ended with 52.31 mm mean seam gap and 250.74% maximum local absolute strain. Initial placement/pins were the raw retained placement, without Fusion native outside recovery. This is one uncalibrated arrangement, not a general comparison of Blender's solver or evidence of vendor parity. It does not justify an improved-quality UI option. The installed files were read-only; generated geometry, scripts and reports stay under ignored `test-output/cloth-api-probe/`.

Original neutral-morph section tracing measures the real Genesis source at 54% / 64% / 74% of its 1.70114 m height: hips 913.825 mm, waist 740.393 mm, chest 950.694 mm. Material-split endpoints are welded at 1 µm; separate closed limb loops are excluded by the torso center test. Inspection evaluates dependencies on an isolated skeleton clone so initializing caches cannot edit the source. The worker, adjustable review and saved snapshots are implemented; anatomical landmarks are not inferred.

A trial warped the retained T-shirt horizontally to independent girths plus 100 mm ease while retaining its old vertical draft and sleeve placement. The resulting 1,962 triangles had minimum angle 26.61°. At 160 × 10 steps/iterations it reached 0.235 mm mean seam gap but 582.54% maximum strain, 16 body/cloth face crossings and two clearance violations (16.76 s). The warp/fit path was removed from distributable source. Closing seams alone is not a sufficient fit gate. The next task must address pattern proportions/arm pose and initial wrapping together rather than treating the scalar width estimate as the only cause.

## Six-panel drafting and pose transfer

A private original six-panel draft uses front/back bodices, four sleeve halves, separate torso girths and upper-arm cross-sections. At 20–40 mm from Genesis's upper-arm joint, a transverse cut still joins the torso; at 60 mm it yields a separate ~316.17 mm arm contour. The prototype uses that separate arm ring plus 80 mm circumference ease and a 140 mm sleeve, with front/back armhole lengths matched independently. It preserves stable v2 segment IDs and source flat rest lengths. This tests a different cut from the earlier two-panel shirt and is not a controlled solver-only comparison.

After correcting the experimental boundary mapping, its 2,154 triangles meet 28.01° minimum angle. The 160 × 10 drape takes 16.45 s: mean seam gap 0.379 mm, max local absolute strain 127.04%, 12 body/cloth face crossings, zero inside vertices and nine clearance violations. Initial recovery moves 306 points. The final front render looks closer to a short-sleeved shirt, but collar/shoulder distortion and numeric failures remain. Neither the source draft nor paid geometry is shipped. Probes and renders stay in ignored `test-output/measured-shirt-probe/`. This is progress in diagnosing pattern/placement conflict, not a fit acceptance result.

The implemented shared-workspace improvement is optional original surface attachment for pose-aware preplacement, described in `docs/progress/2026-10-15-pose-placement.md`. It retains canonical source geometry and flat rest dimensions, checks body identity/connectivity/input SHA, and follows the evaluated skin before the existing XPBD solve. It does not substitute a deformed rest state or claim to solve the remaining pattern strain. Current source and target pose capture are consistent under rigid root transforms. A bounded Blender UV flatten/relax probe on a prepared surface is the next sensible Bridge comparison for developing flatter, lower-distortion panels.
