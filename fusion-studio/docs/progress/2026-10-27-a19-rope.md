# A19 rope / chain / cable — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/rope.js`: Verlet rope with distance-constraint relaxation
(the position-based core also cited for the Vellum-style PBD item).

- `createRope(points, {pinned, gravity, damping, segmentLength})` and
  `createCable(start, end, segments)` (straight, end-pinned).
- `stepRope(rope, dt, {iterations, colliders})`: Verlet integration,
  then relaxation with **alternating sweep direction** — one-way
  Gauss-Seidel measurably biased the chain's symmetry, caught by the
  catenary test and fixed in the solver.
- Sphere colliders push free points out each iteration; pinned points
  are reasserted exactly every iteration; `pinPoint` can re-pin
  mid-simulation; `currentLength` reports live polyline length.
