# A29 sun study & daylight analysis — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/sun-study.js`, composed on the verified solar kernel
(`src/solar.js`, same x-east / y-up / z-south convention):

- `shadowLength(h, elevation) = h / tan(el)` with honest Infinity at
  or below the horizon.
- `isSunlit(point, sunDir, boxes)`: slab ray-cast toward the sun
  against axis-aligned obstacle boxes; points inside an obstacle are
  shaded; below-horizon sun is never sunlit. `sunlitFraction` batches
  facade/point sets.
- `sunStudyDay`: samples sunrise→sunset (from the solar kernel),
  classifies each step, and merges contiguous sunlit/shaded
  intervals with total sun/shade hours; polar day/night returns
  cleanly.
- `sunPath` samples elevation/azimuth/direction over a day.

## Verified (tests/sun-study.test.mjs, 5/5)

Exact shadow-length identities; hand-computed ray cases (low eastern
sun blocked by a wall, steep sun clearing its top, western sun free,
inside-box shading, sideways miss); an equator/equinox day study
whose single morning shade block ends at the analytically derived
9:00 (when `1 + 2·tan(el)` clears the 3 m wall) with shade duration
~3 h; obstacle-free days fully sunlit; polar night; equator noon
elevation > 88°; sunlit fraction 2/3 on a mixed point set.
