# A24 tolerance & clearance — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/clearance.js`, built on the exact closest-point kernel in
`src/mesh-wrap.js`:

- `meshClearance(a, b)`: minimum vertex-to-surface distance in both
  directions, with witness points on each part.
- `clearanceViolations(a, b, tolerance)`: per-part counts of vertices
  inside the tolerance band (the fix-it hot spots).
- `clearanceReport`: min clearance, margin vs the required
  tolerance, pass/fail, and violation counts.

Stated limits (in the API docs and pinned by a test): vertex-to-
surface sampling cannot detect containment or face-face crossings
with no near vertices; a nested cube reports its wall gap (0.75),
not an intersection. Clearance is verified for separated parts —
which is what print/assembly clearance checking is for.

## Verified (tests/clearance.test.mjs, 4/4)

Two cubes 1 unit apart report exactly 1 with witnesses on the facing
surfaces; diagonal separation matches `hypot(1, 2)`; touching faces
report 0; the containment blind spot returns exactly the wall gap;
reports pass at 0.2 requirement (margin +0.1) and fail at 0.5 with
both parts' facing vertices flagged, and none flagged at 0.1.
