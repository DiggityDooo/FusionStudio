# A24 support generation + orientation optimization — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/supports.js`, on the same {positions, indices} mesh shape as the
slicer:

- `overhangFaces`: down-facing faces shallower than a max inclination
  (flat underside = 0°, wall = 90°), with a 1e-4° epsilon so exactly-at-
  threshold faces are not flagged by float32/acos noise.
- `generateSupports`: grid columns under overhangs. Faces are rasterized
  over the XY grid (barycentric cell coverage with interpolated contact
  height; centroid vote as a coverage guarantee) — an earlier
  centroid-only version demonstrably under-covered large triangles.
  Columns rise from the plate to the lowest contact, minus top gap;
  faces resting on the plate generate nothing.
- `rotateMesh` (axis-angle or matrix), `evaluateOrientation`,
  `optimizeOrientation`: candidate orientations dropped onto the plate
  and ranked by support volume, then overhang area.

## Verified (tests/supports.test.mjs, 5/5)

- Flat underside flagged at 0° inclination; a cube/cylinder resting on
  the plate generates zero supports.
- Floating slab: rasterized columns cover its footprint, tops at exactly
  slab bottom minus gap; sphere supports stay in the low shallow band.
- 45° boundary is strict: a 45°-tilted cube prints at threshold 45 and
  is caught at 50.
- Rotation preserves total surface area exactly (208 for a 4×6×8 box).
- A T part (post + cap): as-is needs cap supports; a 180° flip and a 45°
  tilt both reach ~zero — the test asserts the near-zero property and
  the flip specifically rather than a unique argmin, since both are
  legitimate optima under the metric.

Fixture note: feature-tree boxes/cylinders use fan triangulations and a
horizontal cylinder axis, so face-count oracles use clean hand-built
fixtures; that distinction is documented in the tests.
