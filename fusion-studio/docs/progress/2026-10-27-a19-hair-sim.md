# A19 hair/fur strand simulation (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/hair-sim.js`. Tests: `tests/hair-sim.test.mjs`
(3/3).

Guide strands as XPBD particle chains pinned at scalp roots:
distance constraints per segment, uniform gravity plus wind
acceleration, and sphere colliders (head volumes) resolved by
positional projection after each XPBD step with roots re-pinned.

Behavior oracles: a strand starting near-horizontal
pendulum-settles to vertical (probed convergence: max lateral
deviation 0.045 at 3 s, 0.0035 at 6 s — the test settles 6 s)
with root bit-exact and length preserved to <1%; wind deflects
strands downwind with longer strands deflecting strictly
farther; against a sphere collider every particle stays outside
the volume while the strand drapes (length preserved, no NaNs).

Limits: guide strands only (no strand-strand collision, no
bending/twist constraints beyond distance stiffness, static
roots — no scalp animation following), wind is a constant
acceleration field.
