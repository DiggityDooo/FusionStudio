# A22 timecode & genlock sync — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/timecode.js`: frame↔timecode conversion for 23.976–60 fps
including NTSC drop-frame counting, timecode arithmetic, and
half-day-wrapped sync deltas for genlock phase comparison.

Drop-frame follows the standard counting: 29.97 skips frame numbers
00–01 at each minute except every tenth (59.94 skips 00–03).
Nonexistent drop-frame timecodes (e.g. `00:01:00;00`) are rejected
rather than silently remapped.

## Verified (tests/timecode.test.mjs, 6/6)

Spec anchors exactly: frame 1800 = `00:01:00;02`, frame 17982 =
`00:10:00;00` (the 10-minute exception), one drop-frame hour =
107892 frames; round-trips across all eight rates including
250k-frame durations; arithmetic across the drop boundary
(`00:00:59;29` + 1 = `00:01:00;02`); sync deltas wrap at half a day
(23:59:59:24 is −1 frame from midnight); the full error surface.
