# A9 true and vector displacement (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/displace.js`. Tests: `tests/displace.test.mjs`
(3/3). Materials previously carried displacement *settings*
only; this applies displacement to geometry.

`displaceMesh` moves vertices along their normals by
`(height - midpoint) * scale`, with heights from a
`(position, normal, uv, index)` function or a per-vertex array;
`vectorDisplaceMesh` offsets by arbitrary per-vertex vectors;
normals are recomputed after displacement; `displacementRange`
reports the height bounds of a field or array.

Exact oracles: a grid displaced by 0.1x + 0.2z lands on the
function to Float32 precision with x/z untouched, and every
recomputed normal equals the analytic slope normal
normalize(−0.1, 1, −0.2); a sphere displaced radially by 0.25
puts every vertex at radius 1.25 and grows volume by 1.25³
(within tessellation error); (h − midpoint) × scale semantics,
array-vs-function bit equality, and zero-net displacement
identity are asserted directly.

Limits: vertex-density displacement only (no adaptive
tessellation, so silhouettes follow the base mesh), no UV-space
height sampling (the function receives UVs; texture lookup is
the caller's), normals from the shared vertex-normal kernel.
