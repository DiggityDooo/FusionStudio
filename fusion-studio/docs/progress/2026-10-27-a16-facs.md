# A16/A13 FACS expressions & correctives — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/facs.js`: a FACS (Facial Action Coding System) expression
engine.

- 20 action units with names; 8 expression presets (happiness,
  sadness, anger, surprise, fear, disgust, contempt, neutral) as AU
  recipes, including asymmetric contempt ({left, right} weights).
- `composeExpressions`: additive layering with per-side clamping;
  raw AU recipes can be layered alongside named presets.
- `evaluateCorrectives`: combination shapes whose weight is the
  weakest normalized trigger (all triggers must be present),
  with side-specific triggers (`AU12.left`) and scaling.
- `toBlendshapeWeights`: AU → shape mapping with scale and optional
  per-side shape splitting (`shape_L`/`shape_R`).

A latent preset bug was caught in review: `disgust` referenced AU16,
which was missing from the AU table and would have thrown at compose
time. AU16 (lower lip depressor) was added, and a guard test now
composes every shipped preset.

## Verified (tests/facs.test.mjs, 5/5)

Exact preset weights, double-layer clamping, half-weight layers;
asymmetric contempt preserved through blendshape splitting;
correctives at full/weakest-trigger/sub-threshold strengths with
missing-trigger silence; blendshape scaling and absent-AU skipping;
every-preset composition guard; the full validation surface.
