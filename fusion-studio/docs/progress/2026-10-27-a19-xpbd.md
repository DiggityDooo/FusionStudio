# A19 XPBD constraint kernel — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/xpbd.js`, zero-dependency: XPBD (Macklin et al.) worlds with
Float64 positions, inverse masses, distance constraints with compliance in
physical units (m/N), accumulated Lagrange multipliers reset per substep,
Gauss-Seidel iteration, moving pins (infinite-mass anchors), optional ground
plane clamp, and velocity damping. `buildRope` makes pinned chains;
`maxConstraintError` / `chainLength` are solver QA probes.

## Verified (tests/xpbd.test.mjs, 6/6)

- A single rigid constraint is satisfied to 1e-12 with the midpoint
  invariant under symmetric masses.
- A damped 10-segment rope hangs straight: chain length conserved to < 1%,
  free end below the pin, lateral drift < 0.05.
- Compliance is physical: static stretch grows monotonically across
  α = 0 (rigid, < 0.5% stretch), 1e-3, 1e-2 at damped equilibrium.
- Pendulum: at 10° release the period matches 2π√(L/g) within 5% and the
  bob returns to within 0.02 of release height. (At 90° the measured period
  matched the 1.18× elliptic large-amplitude correction, confirming the
  solver follows the nonlinear dynamics — the test now uses the regime the
  closed form describes.)
- Substeps control quality: a 30 m/s sideways yank stretches the rope far
  less with 40 substeps than with 1 (fine < 5%).
- A dropped rope settles on the ground plane with zero penetration.

Test-writing lessons recorded in comments: undamped chains never settle
(statics claims need damping to equilibrium), and small-angle formulas need
small angles.
