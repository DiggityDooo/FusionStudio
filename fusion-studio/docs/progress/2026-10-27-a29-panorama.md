# A29 panorama / 360 equirectangular — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/panorama.js`: equirectangular projection in the solar
convention (x east, y up, z south; north u=0.5, east u=0.75, zenith
v=0).

- `directionToUV` / `uvToDirection` are exact inverses (round-trip
  verified to 1e-9 over scattered directions and UVs). Pole longitude
  is degenerate (`atan2(0, -0)`); u is pinned to 0.5 there
  deterministically — caught by the zenith test.
- `renderPointsEquirect`: a reference splat renderer (one pixel per
  point, nearest depth wins) that verifies the mapping end-to-end
  with occlusion, plus `pixelDirection` for viewer ray generation.

## Verified (tests/panorama.test.mjs, 4/4)

Cardinal directions at documented UVs; bidirectional round-trips;
a rendered 8x4 image where a nearer green point occludes a farther
red one at the exact north pixel (4,2) with depth 2 recorded, east
blue at (6,2), background and Infinity depth preserved elsewhere;
seam continuity across south; zero-direction and size validation.
