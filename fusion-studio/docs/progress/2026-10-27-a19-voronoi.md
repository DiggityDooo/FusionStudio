# A19 Voronoi fracture — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/voronoi.js`, zero-dependency, 2D:

- `computeVoronoi(sites, bounds)`: each cell is the bounds rectangle clipped
  by every perpendicular-bisector half-plane (convex by construction).
  Duplicate sites degrade to an empty cell for the later duplicate.
- `polygonArea` / `polygonCentroid`: shoelace + area-weighted centroid.
- `lloydRelaxation(sites, bounds, iterations)`: sites move to cell
  centroids, evening the distribution.
- `shardPrism(polygon, depth)`: extruded prism mesh; fans are valid because
  Voronoi cells are convex.
- `fracturePlate({width, height, sites, depth})` and deterministic
  `scatterSites(count, bounds, seed)`.

## Verified (tests/voronoi.test.mjs, 5/5)

- Two sites split the unit square exactly along the bisector (areas 0.5/0.5,
  vertices on the correct sides, centroid exact).
- 24 scattered cells tile the bounds to 1e-9 total area, every site inside
  its own convex cell.
- One Lloyd step moves each site exactly onto its cell centroid; 25 steps
  cut the cell-area variance by more than half on a clustered seed set.
- Shard prism signed volume (divergence theorem over the actual triangles)
  equals area × depth per shard, and shards tile the plate volume exactly.
  This test caught an inverted top-fan winding (volume came out at exactly
  1/3), now fixed and explained in the source comment.
- Duplicates: later duplicate gets zero area, remaining cells still tile.
