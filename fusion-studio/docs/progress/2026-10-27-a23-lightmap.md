# A23 UV-space lightmap baking (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/lightmap.js`. Tests: `tests/lightmap.test.mjs`
(4/4). Complements the per-vertex baker in `src/light-bake.js`
with texture-space baking: UV triangles are rasterized at texel
centers, world position and normal are barycentrically
interpolated, and direct lighting (Lambert, inverse-square point
falloff, shadow rays through the existing occlusion/BVH path) is
evaluated per texel. Extra occluder meshes cast shadows without
receiving.

Oracles: a full-UV floor quad under a vertical directional light
bakes to exactly intensity × color at all 256 texels; point-light
texels reproduce the closed-form attenuation × cos law at sampled
texels; an occluder quad produces an exactly black shadowed half
and an exactly lit half under a vertical light; coverage counts
match UV triangle area; the BVH bake is bit-identical to brute
force.

Limits: texel-center sampling (no supersampling or gutter
dilation, so UV island edges can bleed background), overlapping
UV islands overwrite rather than blend, direct lighting only
(no indirect bounces), single texture (no UDIM tiles).
