# A20 interior furniture placement (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/layout.js`. Tests: `tests/layout.test.mjs` (4/4).

Deterministic axis-aligned furniture placement in a rectangular
room: items go largest-first into candidate positions generated
from room, keep-out, and placed-item edges, first-fit in z-then-x
scan order, unrotated before rotated. Keep-out zones (door swings,
radiator strips) are hard constraints; `againstWall` items must
touch a wall; anything that fits nowhere is reported in
`unplaced`. `validateLayout` independently audits a layout for
containment, overlap, keep-out, and wall-contact violations.

Oracles: a hand-computed 3-item room layout is reproduced exactly
(sofa/table/shelf positions derived from the scan order on paper);
the door-swing scenario places the wardrobe edge-on to the
keep-out at exactly (1, 0); rotation is used exactly when the
unrotated footprint cannot fit; a furnished 8-item room validates
clean and is bit-identical across runs.

Limits: rectangular rooms and footprints only (no L-shaped rooms,
no rotated-at-angle placement), first-fit heuristic (valid, not
optimal packing), no clearance/path-width rules beyond keep-outs.
