# Source-surface garment pose placement — 0.17.0

The Clothing workspace adds **Enable pose-aware placement** for an existing garment linked to an imported skinned body. It grades from the neutral source shape, attaches the source arrangement to body triangles, transfers it to the current evaluated skin and then drapes it. **Rebuild pose attachment** repairs an outdated or damaged cache. **Use static source placement** returns to the previous mode. Existing projects retain their current placement choice; neither saved geometry nor reference installations are silently upgraded.

## Original geometry and shared-scene evaluation

The Rust core adds typed `bindGarment` and `placeGarment` requests through the existing disposable geometry helper. Binding uses the existing triangle BVH to find a closest point, records its barycentric coordinates and expresses the residual offset in a triangle frame. Evaluation reconstructs the point from the posed triangle and transports the offset. The normal axis scales with the square root of triangle area, preserving a rigid transform and uniform scale. This is original surface preplacement, not vendor skinning, a cloth solver or certification of garment fit.

Bindings are guarded by stable body identity, vertex/face counts, a deterministic connectivity fingerprint and a SHA-256 of the complete canonical source body, source arrangement, pattern topology, sewing and pins. The native connectivity fingerprint is non-cryptographic; the complete cache-input SHA is checked separately. Malformed barycentrics/offsets, wrong bodies/connectivity, collapsed target triangles and excessive source distances reject with actionable errors. Degenerate source triangles are skipped and counted. The maximum binding distance is 0.5 m in the UI. Native API limits are 60,000 garment points, 200,000 body vertices and 250,000 body triangles; the current character capture/collision path limits body triangles to 200,000.

Source shape and posed skin are captured together on a private skeleton copy. Imported formula/morph/corrective evaluation stays consistent; bone posing is excluded from the source grading measurements. Positive uniform root scale and rigid root rotation/translation are supported. Attached skin matrices are refreshed before collision extraction, fixing double application of rigid root transforms in headless evaluation. Source assets and viewport geometry are unchanged.

Simulation inputs in this optional mode are canonicalized to 0.1 µm to prevent project-roundtrip matrix noise from selecting different contact branches. This includes source attachment coordinates, posed attachment targets, collision coordinates and flat rest positions. It does not round or rewrite the source mesh. Flat pattern distances remain authoritative for strain; a cache does not turn the previous deformed result into a new rest shape. The same source input reuses its attachment, rather than repeatedly baking previous drapes.

Bind, place and drape share one scene-evaluation ticket carrying project/object/revision/input information, and one user transaction. The operator abort signal is combined with the evaluator signal so cancellation during hashing or between native stages cannot start subsequent work. Active native cancellation still waits for actual helper exit. A failed stage retains the prior garment, selection, frame and history. Saved projects retain the cache; standard GLB exchange omits it. New placements remain **Inspired** static-pose previews and require final strain/contact inspection.

## Drafting remains required

A private six-panel measured-shirt prototype uses separate front/back bodices and four sleeve halves, independent torso/arm sections, matched armhole sewing and source-arm placement. Its 2,154 triangles meet a 28.01° minimum angle. At 160 steps × 10 iterations it closes the mean seam gap to 0.379 mm, but maximum local absolute strain is 127.0%, with 12 body/cloth face intersections and 9 clearance violations. The inspected front render shows a more natural short sleeve, but collar/shoulder distortion and the numeric failures prevent acceptance. It is not exposed as an improved template or packaged with private meshes. Generated probes remain under ignored `test-output/measured-shirt-probe/`.

The failed prototype narrows the next task to developable panel shape, collar/shoulder geodesic length and initial surface wrapping. A bounded installed-Blender UV flattening/relaxation comparison is appropriate before adding more ad hoc placement corrections. Measurement-driven drafting/grading, production fit convergence, calibrated material, robust layering/animated contact and fine-mesh drape budgets remain open. Deeper modeling/sculpt/UV, render/sequence controls, broader character authoring and interchange also remain required by the full goal.
