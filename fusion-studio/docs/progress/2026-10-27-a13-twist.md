# A13 twist and corrective joints (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/twist.js`. Tests: `tests/twist.test.mjs` (4/4).

Swing-twist decomposition for the repo's `[w,x,y,z]` quaternions:
`swingTwistDecompose` splits a rotation into swing ∘ twist about
a bone axis (twist extracted by projecting the quaternion vector
part onto the axis), `distributeTwist` splits a twist angle
across helper joints by weights, `twistChainQuaternions` builds
the per-joint twist quaternions, and `correctiveWeight` maps a
bend angle to a smoothstep corrective-shape weight.

Exact oracles (verified through the independent
`quaternionToMatrix` in `src/icp.js`): swing ∘ twist reproduces
the input rotation matrix to 1e-9 on composed/diagonal/170°
cases; the swing remainder is measurably twist-free; the twist
factor leaves its own axis fixed; pure twist and pure swing
measure exactly; 350° wraps to −10°; distributed angles sum
exactly and the helper chain composes to the full twist;
corrective weights hit 0 / 0.5 / 1 exactly at start / midpoint /
full.

Limits: decomposition only (no skinning integration or
corrective shape authoring), twist measured in the bone's local
axis frame supplied by the caller.
