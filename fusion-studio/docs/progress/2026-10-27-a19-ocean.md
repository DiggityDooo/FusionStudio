# A19 FFT ocean — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/ocean.js`, zero-dependency:

- `phillipsSpectrum(omega, k, params)`: P(k) = A (1/k⁴)
  exp(-1/(kL)²) (k̂·ŵ)² with small-wave damping — the standard Tessendorf
  formulation.
- `createOcean({size, divisions, wavelength, windDirection, windSpeed, chop})`:
  deterministic mulberry32 complex Gaussian modes per wave vector.
- `omega(k) = sqrt(g·k)`, deep-water dispersion; per-mode time evolution with
  pair symmetry so the height field stays real.
- `oceanHeightfield(ocean, t)`: exact 2D inverse FFT (radix-2), N ≤ 128.
- `oceanHeightAt(ocean, x, z, t)`: direct modal sum — independent check.

## Verified (tests/ocean.test.mjs, 3/3)

- A single conjugate mode pair produces the analytic standing wave
  `2·cos(θx)` (amplitude 2 from the pair sum), nodes at a quarter period,
  inversion at the half period — IFFT and direct sum agree to Float32 storage
  precision (1e-6).
- A 16×16 random sea: IFFT equals the direct sum with relative error < 1e-6
  (the comparison is relative — heights are stored Float32 like every other
  mesh attribute in the app).
- Phillips spectrum concentrates energy downwind: spectrum power at +x
  exceeds −x by > 50× at the test wavenumber; finite everywhere except the
  exact zero mode.

Two test-expectation fixes during development were traced to the kernel being
right (conjugate-pair doubling) and to Float32 storage, respectively — both
documented in the test.
