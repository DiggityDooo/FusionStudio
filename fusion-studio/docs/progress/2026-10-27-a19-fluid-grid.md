# A19 grid fluid (MAC) solver — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/fluid-grid.js`, zero-dependency, 2D MAC (marker-and-cell) staggered
grid — velocities on cell faces, scalars (density, temperature, pressure,
divergence) at centers:

- `createFluidGrid(size, {spacing})` with `uIndex/vIndex/cellIndex` helpers.
- `maxDivergence(grid)`: exact MAC divergence per cell.
- `projectVelocity(grid, iterations)`: 5-point pressure Poisson (Neumann at
  walls) solved by red-black Gauss-Seidel with SOR (ω = 1.7), gradient
  subtracted at interior faces, wall faces re-pinned to 0.
- `advectField` / internal velocity advection: semi-Lagrangian with bilinear
  sampling (monotone — no overshoot).
- `smokeStep(grid, dt, opts)`: buoyancy (dense sinks, hot rises), advect,
  project, advect scalars.

## Two solver lessons, both caught by tests

1. A collocated central-difference prototype cannot converge: the Nyquist
   (checkerboard) divergence mode is invisible to the central gradient, so
   no pressure field can remove it. This is why MAC staggering exists —
   divergence and gradient are exact adjoints there.
2. Jacobi iteration on the Neumann Poisson problem has a checkerboard error
   mode with amplification exactly −1 (never damps). Red-black Gauss-Seidel
   with SOR replaces it. Separately, wall faces are boundary conditions held
   at 0, not unknowns; randomizing them (as the first test did) makes the
   Neumann problem incompatible — the test now initializes walls to 0, as
   every solver step maintains them.

## Verified (tests/fluid-grid.test.mjs, 5/5)

- Random interior velocity field: max |divergence| reduced from O(1) to
  < 1e-6 (five-plus orders of magnitude).
- A discrete-curl (streamfunction) field is exactly divergence-free and the
  projection leaves it unchanged (< 1e-9).
- Semi-Lagrangian advection translates a density blob's centroid by exactly
  the flow displacement, with near-conserved mass.
- Hot smoke rises over 30 steps, density never exceeds its initial maximum
  (monotone advection), and the per-step divergence residual stays < 1e-3.
