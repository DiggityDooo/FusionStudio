# A19 granular (sand) — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/granular.js`: 2D position-based granular simulation.

- Uniform disk grains in a walled container (open top), spatial-hash
  broadphase, positional non-penetration projection over solver
  iterations and substeps.
- Friction is **velocity-level**: once per substep, contacting pairs
  lose a friction-scaled share of tangential relative velocity, and
  floor grains lose horizontal speed. An earlier draft applied
  positional friction inside every projection iteration, which
  over-damped sliding ~16× per step and froze piles into vertical
  towers (measured 78° binned slope); the test caught it and the
  model was restructured, not the oracle.
- `pourGrains` (seeded jitter), `kineticEnergy`, and
  `surfaceProfile` (per-bin max grain top) support measurement.

## Verified (tests/granular.test.mjs, 4/4)

A 104-grain pour settles (rest energy < 2% of peak) with every grain
inside the container; worst pairwise overlap < 15% of diameter at
rest; the settled pile is a genuine heap — peak 0.80 vs 0.18
stray-grain edges, envelope repose angle inside (8°, 50°) (test
scenarios were twice corrected when the *box* was the problem: too
few grains to heap in a wide box, then a box filled wall-to-wall); a
single grain falls, lands exactly at its radius, and stays at rest.
