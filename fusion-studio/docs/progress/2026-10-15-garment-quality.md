# Garment quality meshing and solid-body contact — 0.14.0

## User-visible behavior

**Inspired:** version-2 pattern meshes now seed panel interiors and legalize unconstrained edges locally before bounded refinement/smoothing. Stable boundary parameters, partial seam correspondence, holes, internal lines, material groups and pins stay constrained. The preview reports a 20° minimum-angle target and whether it is met. Acute authored corners and narrow constraint spacing can make the target impossible; they keep warnings rather than receive a false quality label. New live T-shirts use version-2 documents. Existing projects and version-1 pattern meshes remain readable and are not silently converted on load.

Imported-body fitting now recovers initial points embedded in a closed body using nearest-surface queries and ray parity. The compatibility notice explains that automatic placement can adjust panel/pin positions before pin targets are locked. Original 2D panels stay retained; pinned positions do not drift during the subsequent solve. Face-intersection response supplements vertex/face sweeps, so a body passing through a cloth face can be detected even when all three cloth vertices are outside. The live panel reports body/cloth face intersections, cloth vertices inside the body and initial placement adjustments separately from local surface-clearance counts. Any remaining intersections or inside vertices make fit diagnostics unconverged.

These are owned algorithms. The mathematical background is [constrained Delaunay refinement](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/tripaper/triangle3.html) and [XPBD](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/XPBD.pdf). No Triangle library, vendor solver or decompiled pseudocode is copied or distributed. Fusion's bounded sampler is not a proof of Ruppert-style termination or a universal angle guarantee.

## Numeric gates and limitations

- The detailed partial-seam fixture now has 23,430 triangles and 11,957 vertices at 11 mm particle distance, minimum angle 29.07°, maximum edge 10.94 mm. All constraints remain actual edges, boundary-edge counts match authored chains without hanging edges, and material-split panels remain separate. The historical 18 mm fixture used 24,616 triangles yet had a 4.79° minimum angle; the new mesher avoids that over-refined skinny layout. These use different particle distances to retain comparable detailed surface sizes; they are not equal-resolution timing claims.
- Curved boundaries retain their parameter coordinates; holes stay empty; internal lines and darts survive. Meshing is deterministic. Acute input corners explicitly fail the 20° target. Worker preview remains responsive and cancellable while detailed meshing runs.
- A 16,128-triangle thin sphere collider and 5,000-triangle cloth recover 145 embedded initial vertices, with zero final inside vertices, strict face intersections and clearance violations. Pins remain fixed. Reversed body winding has the same parity/placement result in native tests. A separate crossing fixture proves outside cloth vertices cannot hide a body piercing a face.
- The owned 50,312-triangle Genesis probe uses a 2,422-triangle/1,304-vertex garment, minimum angle 32.43°. At 160 steps/10 iterations it reports zero strict body/cloth face crossings and zero inside vertices, but about 104% maximum local absolute strain, 1.9% mean strain, 6.95 mm mean seam gap and one clearance violation. Solver time is about 16–18 seconds on this PC. **It fails production-fit acceptance.**

Ray parity assumes a closed body. Open surfaces, overlapping/nested components and numerical edge cases can yield ambiguous classifications. Intersection tests detect strict triangle crossings; coplanar/grazing configurations still depend on clearance and swept contact and are not a complete collision proof. Layered cloth, robust animated contact, material calibration and converged sewing remain unfinished. Existing two-sided contact is retained for non-solid colliders. Only imported live-body fitting enables solid placement/face response automatically, after its compatibility notice.

Limits remain 200,000 pattern triangles, bounded local flips, 250,000 sample-grid candidates and 40 million point-location steps per panel. Native cloth remains bounded at 60,000 vertices, 100,000 faces, 240 steps and 24 iterations. Geometry requests time out after 30 seconds; this bounds the pending request, not the CPU time of a synchronous native call already running in a worker thread. A cancellation probe rejected after 1.04 seconds but the native worker exited at 14.40 seconds. Cooperative native cancellation or killable process isolation is required next. Large detailed panels can also block synchronous live-fit preparation for seconds; the separate pattern preview uses a worker. Fine full drapes can exceed the request limit. No interactive or MD-equivalent quality claim follows from these gates.
