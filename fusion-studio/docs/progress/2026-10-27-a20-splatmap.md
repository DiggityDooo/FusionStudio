# A20 terrain splat painting (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/splatmap.js`. Tests: `tests/splatmap.test.mjs` (3/3).

Rule-based terrain texture layers over the existing heightfield
convention (flat `res²` heights, world-unit extent):
`heightfieldSlopes` computes per-texel slope angles by central
differences; `splatWeights` evaluates per-layer height/slope band
rules with smoothstep falloffs and per-layer strength, normalizes
each texel, and assigns any uncovered remainder to a base layer,
so weights sum to exactly 1 everywhere. `splatDominantLayer` and
`splatCoverage` provide the preview map and coverage audit.

Oracles: slope is exactly atan(0.2) on a tilted plane (central
differences are exact on linear fields, borders included via
one-sided differences) and π/4 on a cone flank; hard (zero-falloff)
bands are exactly 0/1 at the band extremes; a slope-gated layer
never fires below its slope threshold; falloff blending is
monotone; uncovered texels fall back to the base layer at exactly
weight 1.

Limits: rule-based auto-painting only — no manual brush painting,
no splat texture export, no triplanar/detail blending.
