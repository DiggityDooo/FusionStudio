# A22 live compositing & chroma keying — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/chroma-key.js`: excess-channel chroma keyer with despill,
premultiply, and over-compositing.

- Keyness = pixel's dominant-channel excess normalized by the key
  color's own excess (works for green/blue/any saturated key; a gray
  key is rejected as unkeyable). Alpha is a smoothstep over
  `[similarity - blend, similarity]` — soft edges, exact endpoints.
- Despill caps the key channel at the max of the other two channels
  (full or partial strength), removing green fringe on edges.
- `chromaKeyImage` (RGB/RGBA in, RGBA out), `premultiply`, and
  `compositeOver` complete the live-comp path.

## Verified (tests/chroma-key.test.mjs, 5/5)

Pure key → alpha 0; gray/red → alpha 1; blue-key symmetry; half-

monotonicity; despill arithmetic at full/half strength with

backdrop keyed out, subject opaque and unaltered, spilled edge
semi-transparent and despilled, composite showing background/subject
in the right places, premultiply invariants.
