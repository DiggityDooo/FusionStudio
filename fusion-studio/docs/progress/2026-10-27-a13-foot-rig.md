# A13 reverse foot / foot roll — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/foot-rig.js`: the classic three-phase foot-roll rig driven
by one `roll` parameter plus `bank`.

- roll ∈ [-1, 0]: heel pivot; [0, 0.5]: ball pivot; [0.5, 1]: toe
  pivot. Bank rotates about the inner/outer ground edge.
- Phases **compose cumulatively**: the toe phase rotates about the
  toe's current position after the ball rotation. A naive
  pivot-swap implementation pops the ankle at the 0.5 boundary — the
  continuity oracle caught that, and the solver was restructured to
  ordered rotation steps. (An earlier sign inversion, which drove the
  heel through the floor, was likewise caught by the lift oracles.)

## Verified (tests/foot-rig.test.mjs, 5/5)

Rest pose is the identity; the active ground contact stays fixed in
world space per phase (toe contact compared at its phase-start
position); the ankle trajectory matches independent pivot-rotation
trigonometry to 1e-12; pitch and ankle position are continuous across
both phase boundaries; full roll lifts the heel clear and heel-roll
lifts the toe; parameters clamp to [-1, 1].
