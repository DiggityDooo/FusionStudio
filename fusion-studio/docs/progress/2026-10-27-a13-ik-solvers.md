# A13 FABRIK + spline IK solvers — 2026-10-27

## Context

The ledger's IK-solver item names spline, spring, multi-chain and HIK,
but `src/ik.js` implements the (well-tested) two-bone/scene path only —
no spline or multi-joint chain solver existed. New pure-math
`src/ik-solvers.js` fills the spline and multi-chain entries:

- `solveFabrik(jointPositions, target, opts)`: classic FABRIK with
  lengths taken from the input chain; unreachable targets stretch the
  chain straight at the target.
- `catmullRomPoint` / `catmullRomLength`: uniform Catmull-Rom with
  numerical arc length.
- `splineIk(controlPoints, segmentLengths, {initialNormal})`: joints
  placed by arc length along the curve (binary search on the monotone
  arc-length function) with parallel-transport frames — the normal is
  carried by tangent-change rotations only, so the frame field itself
  introduces no twist.

## Verified (tests/ik-solvers.test.mjs, 6/6)

- FABRIK: end effector within 1e-3 of a reachable target, segment
  lengths preserved to 1e-9, root pinned, input unmutated; unreachable
  target gives a fully extended collinear chain.
- Catmull-Rom passes through every control point; a straight control
  polygon gives exact length and midpoint.
- Spline IK on a line: joints at exact arc spacings with axial tangents;
  chain longer than curve throws.
- Spline IK on a circular arc (9 controls, R = 10): joints stay on the
  circle (±0.05) and chords match 2R sin(arc/2R).
- Parallel transport on a helix-like spline: normals unit and
  perpendicular to tangents, no frame flips (max turn 0.6 rad), the
  requested initial normal is honored, and a straight spline's frame is
  exactly constant.
