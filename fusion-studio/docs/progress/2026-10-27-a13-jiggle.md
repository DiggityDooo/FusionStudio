# A13 jiggle / secondary motion — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/jiggle.js`: damped harmonic oscillators for secondary motion,
parameterized by natural frequency `omega` and damping ratio `zeta`
so behavior is the analytic oscillator response.

- `createSpring`: scalar spring with internal substepping
  (`omega*dt/0.05` steps) — stiff springs stay stable at 20 fps.
- `createJiggle`: 3D point whose springs live in **world space** and
  chase `anchor + restOffset` (an earlier offset-space draft could
  never be excited by anchor motion; the lag test caught it). Gravity
  sags the target. The visible secondary motion is `point - anchor`.
- `createJiggleChain`: ponytail/antenna chains where each link chases
  the previous point.

## Verified (tests/jiggle.test.mjs, 6/6)

Against closed-form oscillator theory: step-response overshoot
matches `1 + exp(-pi*zeta/sqrt(1-zeta^2))` within 3% at zeta 0.2;
overdamped (zeta 1, 1.5) responses are monotone and overshoot-free;
measured oscillation period matches `2pi/(omega*sqrt(1-zeta^2))`
within 2%; an anchor step excites lateral lag that decays back to the
rest offset; gravity sag lands at the exact offset; chains propagate
bounded swings; stiff springs converge at coarse frame rates.
