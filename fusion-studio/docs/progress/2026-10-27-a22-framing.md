# A22 blocking/previz framing analysis (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/framing.js`. Tests: `tests/framing.test.mjs` (3/3).

Screen-space framing metrics for blocking a subject through a
solved camera (repo `projectPoint` convention, v downward):
projected subject bbox and center, frame coverage, distance to
the nearest rule-of-thirds intersection, headroom above a head
point, clipping detection, threshold warnings, camera horizon
roll from the rotation's right-vector, and a closed-form dolly
factor for hitting a target coverage (distance scales with the
square root of the coverage ratio).

Exact oracles: with P = K[I|0] the constructed point (1.6, 1.2,
−10) projects exactly onto the (W/3, H/3) thirds intersection
(thirds distance 0, headroom exactly 1/3); a constructed quad
covers exactly 1/9 of the frame; a 10° rolled camera reads
exactly 10°; quadrupling coverage needs a dolly factor of
exactly 2.

Limits: single subject point set (no multi-character balance or
eyeline/180°-rule checks), warning thresholds are defaults the
caller can override, no rendered overlay.
