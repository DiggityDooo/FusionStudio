# A10 Monte Carlo irradiance and ambient occlusion (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/gi-sample.js`. Tests: `tests/gi-sample.test.mjs`
(3/3).

The sampling core of a GI/irradiance system: cosine-weighted
hemisphere sampling (Malley's method) with the matching
cos/π pdf, an irradiance estimator driven by a caller-supplied
occlusion predicate, and an analytic ray-sphere occluder.

Oracles: on an open uniform sky the estimator is exactly
π·radiance with zero variance (each cosine-weighted sample
contributes cos/pdf = π identically — this pins the sampler/pdf
pairing); a sky with exactly half its cosine-weighted measure
blocked integrates to AO 0.5 within the estimator's own reported
standard error, and the error shrinks like 1/√N between sample
counts; a sphere subtending a known half-angle gives
AO = 1 − sin²(half-angle) = 0.75 for r/dist = 1/2, again within
reported error. All RNG is the seeded `mulberry32`, so runs are
deterministic.

Limits: single-bounce direct sky irradiance/AO only (no path
tracing, no irradiance-cache interpolation structure, no photon
mapping), occlusion is a callback rather than scene traversal.
