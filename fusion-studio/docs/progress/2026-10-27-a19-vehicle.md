# A19 vehicle & wheel physics — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/vehicle.js`: dynamic bicycle model with linear-range tires
saturated per axle at `mu * static load`, friction-capped drive and
brake forces, quadratic drag and rolling resistance.

Development defects caught by the oracles, fixed in the kernel:
- Tire-force sign was reversed relative to the slip-angle definitions;
  cornering diverged into oscillation until the convention was made
  consistent (`Fy = +C * alpha` for the definitions used).
- Drive force was not friction-capped longitudinally; it is now
  clamped to `mu * m * g`, and the launch test asserts a huge engine
  cannot beat the cap.

## Verified (tests/vehicle.test.mjs, 5/5)

- Steady cornering converges to within the dynamic understeer band of
  the kinematic bicycle (`yaw = v * tan(steer) / L`, measured ratio
  ~0.94 at 10 m/s).
- Full braking from 30 m/s stops within [0.95, 1.25] × the
  friction-limit distance `v²/(2 mu g)`.
- Launch acceleration with a 1e9 N engine stays at the `mu g` cap.
- High-speed cornering: sampled tire forces reach but never exceed
  `mu g` laterally, and saturated yaw stays well below kinematic
  demand. (At sustained excessive steer the model settles into a
  saturated drift state — noted, not hidden.)
- Straight-line driving has zero lateral drift; wheel angular speed
  matches rolling `vx / r`.
