# A15 NLA evaluation kernel — 2026-10-27

## Context

`src/animation.js` has NLA track/clip data structures (with a source-frame
mapping helper) but no evaluator that mixes a track stack into values.
New pure `src/nla-eval.js` provides the mixing semantics:

- Clip strips map timeline → source frames with a time scale
  (sourceLength/stripLength) and wrap per repeat; activity is [start, end).
- Envelopes: explicit fadeIn/fadeOut, else derived from overlaps with
  neighbouring clips on the same track (0 = hard cut), scaled by clip weight.
- Override clips on a track mix by normalized weights; track influence is
  min(1, Σweights) × track weight over lower tracks' accumulated value.
- Additive clips contribute (sample − reference) × envelope × weight,
  reference defaulting to the rest value.
- The clip sampler is injected (`sampleClip(clip, sourceFrame)`), keeping
  the kernel independent of the keyframe representation.

## Verified (tests/nla-eval.test.mjs, 6/6)

- Time mapping: 2× stretch lands on exact source frames; repeat wraps
  (frame 25 of a 3×10 strip = source 5) and extends activity.
- Single clip = its sampled value; empty/inactive stack = rest value.
- Overlap crossfade: midpoint of a 0/10 clip pair is exactly 5; quarter
  point matches the normalized-envelope mix.
- Upper track at weight 0.5 blends 2 → 6 against the lower track; muted
  tracks are skipped.
- Additive offsets apply against explicit and rest-default references.
- Explicit fades × clip weight produce exact influence ramps.
