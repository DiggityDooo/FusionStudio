# A24 lattice generation — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/lattice.js`: beam lattices filling a box volume as merged
prism meshes (`grid` cube-edge cells and `bcc` corner-to-center
cells), plus a relative-density estimator using the exact polygon
cross-section. Density is documented as an upper bound: joint
overlaps are counted per-strut, and struts are not trimmed to the
volume (boundary struts bleed one radius past the box).

Development defect caught by the volume oracle: prism side faces
were wound inward, making the divergence-theorem volume exactly
-1/3 of the true value (inverted sides, correct caps). Fixed and
re-verified per-strut.

## Verified (tests/lattice.test.mjs, 5/5)

Exact strut counts (54 for a 2x2x2 grid, 8/64 for BCC) confirmed by
union-find component counting -- one component per prism; vertex/
index counts per prism; total strut lengths including BCC diagonals
(8*sqrt(3)); mesh divergence volume equal to the analytic strut
volume within 1% at 32-gon resolution; density scaling as r^2 and
decreasing with cell size; validation errors.
