# A15 lip sync (phoneme → viseme) — 2026-10-27

## Kernel

New `src/lip-sync.js`: timed phonemes to viseme weight curves.

- A 39-phoneme table maps to 14 visemes (PP/FF/TH/DD/KK/CH/SS/NN/RR
  consonant families, AA/E/I/O/U vowels, sil).
- Coarticulation is a raised-cosine dominance window: each phoneme
  reaches weight 1 across its interval and bleeds `coarticulation`
  seconds into both neighbors (anticipation before onset is the
  visible effect); visemes blend by maximum dominance.
- `createLipSync` returns sampled frames at a chosen fps, `sampleAt`
  for arbitrary times, and `leadAt` for the dominant viseme. Silence
  rests at `sil = 1` outside utterances. Input is sorted; overlaps,
  unknown phonemes, and non-positive durations are explicit errors.

## Verified (tests/lip-sync.test.mjs, 5/5)

A five-phoneme "hello" leads with the right viseme at each phoneme
center at full weight; every frame carries every viseme in [0, 1];
anticipation is present at 0.06 s coarticulation, absent at 0, and
monotone through the rise window; boundary blends mix both neighbors;
gaps rest at silence; the phoneme table's family groupings; the full
validation surface.
