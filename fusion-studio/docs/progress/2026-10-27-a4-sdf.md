# A4 SDF / volume modeling kernel (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/sdf.js`. Tests: `tests/sdf.test.mjs` (5/5).
Evidence added for `A4 Volumes, VDB and SDF modeling` and
`A4 Field-based operations`.

Exact signed-distance primitives (negative inside): sphere, box,
rounded box, torus, capsule, capped cylinder, ground plane. CSG:
union/intersection/subtraction, polynomial smooth-min union (the
symmetry-plane value d − k/4 is asserted exactly), and shells.
Domain ops: translate, uniform scale (distances scale exactly),
rotation, and infinite repetition (with the standard
boundary-approximation caveat documented). Queries: gradient
normals and sphere tracing, which lands on analytic surfaces
(sphere and box hits at t = 4 exactly). `sampleSdfGrid` produces
a dense voxel sample (explicitly not a sparse VDB tree), and
`sdfToMesh` extracts the surface through the existing
marching-tetrahedra polygonizer with gradient normals.

Volume oracles: extracted sphere volume within 4% of 4π/3 at
res 36; two-sphere union within 5% of the analytic lens formula
(2·4π/3 − 5π/12); subtraction strictly shrinks the base volume;
every extracted vertex lies on the zero level (|sdf| < 0.03).

Two oracle bugs were caught and fixed during testing (kernel was
right both times): the rounded-box center distance is −min(half),
not −(min(half) − r), because the shape is a Minkowski sum; and
Float32 grid storage caps node precision at ~1e-6.

Limits: dense grids only (no sparse VDB tree, no level-set
advection), repetition distances approximate near cell borders,
no SDF texturing/attributes yet.
