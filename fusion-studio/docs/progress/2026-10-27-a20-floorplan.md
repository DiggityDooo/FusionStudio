# A20 floor plan to 3D conversion (2026-10-27)

Kernel: `src/floorplan.js`. Tests: `tests/floorplan.test.mjs` (3/3).

`buildFloorPlan(walls)` extrudes 2D wall segments (from/to in XZ,
height, thickness) into 3D walls with door/window openings. Each wall
decomposes into full-height runs, sills (below windows), and lintels
(above openings); pieces are emitted as boxes in the wall frame with
constant per-face normals. `floorPlanVolume` gives the analytic
volume for auditing.

Exact oracles:
- Mesh divergence volume equals analytic boxes-minus-voids volume for
  a wall with a door + window (0.9×2.1 and 1.5×1.2 voids).
- Piece decomposition is exactly [run, lintel, run, sill, lintel,
  run]; no piece intrudes into either opening void.
- Mesh bounds equal the wall extent.
- Validation rejects openings outside the wall, overlapping
  openings, over-height openings, and zero-length walls.

Limits: overlapping corner volumes between joined walls are not
accounted in the analytic volume (per-wall accounting); no wall
joinery/mitering, no multi-story stacking, no UI.
