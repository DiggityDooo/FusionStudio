Current strand variants and starter coverage are previews. No new release is packaged. See [implemented scope, evidence and remaining gates](docs/progress/2026-10-23-character-creator.md).

## Development side effect

A copied-runtime DAZ probe exported Genesis 9 and started shared CMS. It was retired. Its unused runtime remains in the workspace after optional deletion was blocked by automatic approval review; runtime/helper are excluded from packaging. The delivered DSON route never launches DAZ/CMS. Install/selected asset checks remain unchanged; the audit does not cover shared CMS/OS activity.

Remaining work is prioritized by the standing brief: garment meshing/contact/grading/animated collision after the verified character foundation, then modeling depth, rendering and interchange. Broader character controls and unsupported dependency bindings remain open. Unsupported native formats and conversion limits appear before processing; native CC/MD parsing is not required to claim progress in the owned core workflow.
